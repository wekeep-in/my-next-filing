# Ordinary domestic dividends and additional interest

Reviewed 6 September 2026 for Tax Year 2026-27, 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. This is a narrow extension to one supported presumptive freelance practice under the new regime. It does not establish eligibility for a particular return form.

## Decision

Proceed with four annual amounts, separate from bank interest and freelance receipts: ordinary Indian-company dividends, taxable Indian mutual-fund distributions, taxable post-office interest, and Indian income-tax refund interest. Require an explicit scope and amount confirmation. Unknown amounts, uncertain tax treatment, or unsupported income must not become zero.

The amounts must come from tax records for this year, before TDS. They are not necessarily the sum of bank credits. Keep one combined actual Indian TDS input. No investment names, folio numbers, PANs, transaction records, or documents are needed.

## Amount contract and exclusions

The proposed controls should accept only the following confirmed annual tax amounts. These are implementation boundaries, not claims that every excluded case is legally prohibited.

| Amount | Accepted contract | Exclude or require review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Indian-company dividends | Ordinary dividends taxable for this year, before TDS and expenses. Use the issuer's tax records to resolve the year. | Foreign-company income, deemed dividends, company loans, liquidation/capital reductions, buybacks, or uncertain classification. |
| Indian mutual-fund distributions | Taxable IDCW from an Indian regulated mutual fund, before TDS, including distributions reinvested into units. | Sale, redemption, switching, capital gains/losses, foreign funds, REIT/InvIT/business trusts, AIF pass-through income, or unresolved capital/distribution classification. |
| Taxable post-office interest | Confirmed taxable interest for this year before TDS. The record must already resolve any applicable exemption and ownership share. | Principal, full maturity proceeds, exempt amounts, unresolved exemptions, special certificates/bonds, premature-closure tax adjustments, or deduction/expense claims. |
| Income-tax refund interest | The interest component taxable this year, separately shown in the refund/tax record. | Refund principal, GST or other refund interest, reversed/disputed interest, cross-year adjustments, or unresolved ownership/year treatment. |

The [SEBI circular of 5 October 2020, SEBI/HO/IMD/DF3/CIR/P/2020/194](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2020/circular-on-review-of-dividend-option-s-plan-s-in-case-of-mutual-fund-schemes_47795.html) renamed dividend plans as income distribution cum capital withdrawal, with payout, reinvestment and transfer options. The [issued PDF](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/oct-2020/1601906688276.pdf) explains that a distribution can draw from equalisation reserves. A cash-only label would omit reinvestments; an instruction to subtract the capital part from IDCW would also be unsafe. Ask for the confirmed taxable distribution amount.

The Income Tax Department's [ITR-4 FAQ](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/itr%204-faqs), question 1, lists post-office deposit interest and income-tax refund interest among other-source categories. It concerns AY 2026-27 and corroborates terminology only, not this tax year's form eligibility. The [AIS handbook, February 2024, section 4.7](https://static.insight.gov.in/resources/pdf/AIS%20Handbook_v3.0.0.pdf) identifies refund interest as taxable other-source income; its search-indexed text was readable, but direct retrieval failed. Use the enacted Act for operative treatment, not the handbook's old section references.

No post-office exemption amount is coded in this slice. The notification behind the familiar savings-account exemption was not retrieved from an official host during this review. Unresolved exemptions must fail the scope confirmation. Similarly, the app should not calculate interest from a scheme's rate or maturity proceeds. A generic field for all interest would silently expand into lending, securities and compensation cases.

## GST separation

Keep GST aggregate turnover as the existing independent, explicitly confirmed PAN-wide amount. Do not add the four new fields to it, and do not apply an arithmetic equality check against income-tax totals.

[CGST Act sections 2(6), 2(52), 2(101), 2(102) and 7](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/CGST-Act-Updated-31082021.pdf) define turnover by supplies and exclude securities from goods/services. Ordinary passive shareholder distributions are not consideration for a supply by the shareholder, so they should not be copied into turnover. This is a legal inference for the narrow confirmed ownership case, not a blanket rule about every investment receipt. Related service fees are different. The [CBIC banking and stock-broker FAQs, questions 34 and 89](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/sectoral-faq.html) corroborate the distinction between securities and related services.

[Notification 12/2017 Central Tax Rate, dated 28 June 2017, entry 27](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/pdf/central-tax-rate/Notification12-CGST.pdf) exempts qualifying deposit-interest services from GST. Exempt supplies can still enter registration turnover under section 2(6). The post-office income-tax field therefore cannot establish the GST amount: a tax exemption can change one without changing the other.

Income-tax refund interest is a statutory payment, not automatically a deposit-interest supply. Do not tell users to add every interest amount indiscriminately. This review did not retrieve a nationwide ruling specifically on its GST treatment, so retain the direct complete-turnover declaration and ask for review if the person cannot establish that amount. Do not add a new global exclusion merely because confirmed refund interest exists.

The accessible CBIC compilation is dated 31 August 2021. The newer India Code compilation was located but failed to open. No GST valuation formula or threshold change is proposed in this income slice.
