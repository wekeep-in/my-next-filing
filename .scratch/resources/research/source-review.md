## Search and period review

ITR and income tax return describe the same retrieval topic; LUT and letter of undertaking are equivalent retrieval phrases. GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B remain distinct. Form, section, notification and year tokens are not fuzzy-matched. The [official transition FAQ](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/tax-payments-faq?mobile-app=1) distinguishes Assessment Year from the new Tax Year, supporting the explanatory empty state instead of automatic period conversion. Explicit Tax Year phrases constrain recorded statutory coverage; portals receive no invented period.
