## Speaker script

### 1. When is my next tax filing?

> When is my next tax filing?

Pause. Let the audience answer it in their heads. Only the question appears on a solid dark background.

### 2. Compliance is complicated.

> A tax system has to work for people with very different lives. All that complexity needs structure. But when I arrive with one question about my own situation, I still have to find my way through it.

### 3. Terminology cloud

> First, you have to learn another language.

Let the cloud do the work. These terms represent the complexity encountered, not a claim that the app supports every term shown.

### 4. Portal screenshots

> Then you have to work out where to go. And which of all these instructions actually applies to you.

These are public, signed-out screenshots of Income Tax, GST, PAN services and their help pages, captured at varied browser sizes. No portal interaction occurs in the presentation.

### 5. But, what if tax planning was quick and easy?

> What if a tool could take your situation and turn it into a plan? What you might owe, what is due next, and why it applies. That was the starting point for My Next Filing.

### 6. My Next Filing

Only the product name appears, centered.

> That's My Next Filing. A tax plan that starts with your situation, for supported solo freelancers in India. These recordings use fictional details.

### 7. Demo: the person behind the income

> Freelancing describes your work. It doesn't describe your whole income. This person has clients, but also salary, interest, rent and Indian equity gains. The questions collect the supported details together, with conditions for each category. The goal is to ask the questions that matter for this person's plan.

Follow clients, salary, interest, rent and equity. Pause with Space if you want to explain a detail.

### 8. Demo: from answers to a plan

> The plan starts with the next action, its date and the estimated amount left. I can open why it applies, inspect the official sources, and see the calculation. The agenda brings the other supported actions together and flags areas needing review. I still file and pay on the official portals. This gets me there knowing what I'm there to do.

Follow calculate, action, why, sources, calculation and agenda. Do not make unrestricted coverage claims.

### 9. Demo: come back and see what remains

> I can choose to save in this browser and return to my plan. After paying outside the app, I update the amount paid and recalculate. Then I record completion, and the next action changes. That is my record. The app cannot verify government acceptance. I can return without putting the whole picture together again.

Follow save, return, payment update and completion. Completion requires a saved workspace and remains user-declared.

### 10. No compromises on privacy.

Only this centered serif list appears:

- 💻 No account registration.
- 🔌 No portal connection
- 📊 No web analytics
- 🛡️ No compromises on privacy.

> Privacy drove some limits. There is no account or cross-device sync. Browser saving is optional, and clearing that data can erase it. There is no connection to government systems, so the app cannot file, pay or verify completion. Others using the same browser profile may access saved data.

### 11. Built for freelancers by a freelancer.

> Built for freelancers by a freelancer. I wanted a tool I could come back to between doing the work.

Product story ends here.

## Design and evidence

Use the existing Fraunces/Inter system with a low-density stage layout. Design dials are variance 5, motion 2, density 1. The revised treatment uses solid dark backgrounds in place of gradients. Centered single-statement slides use smaller, 64px type. The muted slides provide pauses; the word cloud and real portal collage build tension; dark slides mark the possibility and the personal close. The Codex divider uses a Magic UI terminal adapted to the Remotion frame clock.

Reference decks and screenshot provenance are recorded in `docs/pitch.md`. Product claims come from current SPEC.md and the existing recorded journey. The product demonstrations use fictional details. The terminal illustrates the build process. Neither is evidence of independent tax certification or outside user validation.
