# Employer NPS with salary and freelance income

Reviewed 7 September 2026 for Tax Year 2026-27, from 1 April 2026 through 31 March 2027. This extends the existing resident, ordinarily resident adult individual, new-regime, one-presumptive-practice branch. No retirement-fund valuation or return-form selection is proposed.

## Decision

Support ordinary employer contributions to the person's NPS Tier I account, with a separate contribution and eligible-salary amount for each contributing employer. Keep the existing aggregate salary input. It must include employer NPS contributions once and remain before the standard deduction and employer-NPS deduction. Do not add the contribution to that input again during calculation.

Require a specific confirmation that total employer contributions across recognised PF, NPS and approved superannuation funds, across all employers for the year, do not exceed ₹7,50,000. Also require no taxable annual accretion from earlier or current excess contributions and no unresolved fund-tax adjustments. These are deliberately narrow product exclusions. Exceeding the fund threshold is not itself a legal prohibition on claiming employer NPS.

## Calculation and input contract

Suggested minimum input structure is an explicit none, uncertain or contributions branch. Contributions contains the scope confirmation and employer rows. Rows need no employer name, PAN, PRAN or account identifiers.

- Each row records this employer's contribution for this tax year and its corresponding basic pay plus qualifying DA. Exclude employee-funded contributions even if payroll remits them. Exclude balances, returns, transfers and withdrawals.
- Include every contributing employer once. A later employer's records may repeat earlier employment amounts, so do not copy both combined and individual figures.
- Gross salary covers all employers and includes these contributions. The summed contribution and eligible salary cannot exceed that salary amount together. This check is a product consistency guard for the narrow basic-plus-DA contract, not a complete payroll reconciliation.
- Apply 14% to eligible salary, not gross salary, CTC, freelance receipts or total income. There is no government/private selector needed in a new-regime-only product.
- Calculate each row's deduction as the lesser of contribution and 14% of eligible salary. Sum the row deductions, then cap the combined deduction at gross total income.
- Gross total income is supported presumptive income plus salary after its single standard deduction plus supported other income. Subtract the employer deduction before the existing total-income rounding, slab tax, rebate, marginal relief, cess and credits.
- Preserve the pre-employer-deduction amount for the annual-return income test. The post-deduction rounded amount controls the supported taxable-income ceiling and tax calculation.
- Preserve actual TDS once in the existing combined credit input. Keep all salary and contribution amounts out of freelance receipts and the independently declared GST turnover.

The per-employer cap is an interpretation of section 124(1), which connects the deduction to the contributing employer. I did not find an official worked example deciding whether unused salary capacity from one employer can shelter another employer's over-cap contribution. Do not claim that an official FAQ expressly establishes the per-employer formula. Per-employer records avoid silently using salary from a noncontributing employer. For a stricter first release with no reliance on this unresolved pooling interpretation, support only records whose contribution is within 14% for each employer, and refer over-cap/mixed cases for review.

Personal NPS contributions can exist without creating a new-regime deduction. The product should say they are not deducted, rather than imply that merely having made one disqualifies a person. Keep claims for personal NPS deductions, Tier II tax treatment, Vatsalya, UPS-specific treatment, withdrawals, transfers, pension payouts, Agniveer and other unsupported deductions outside this slice.

## Checks to carry into implementation

Use independent expected amounts, not the production evaluator, for these scenarios.

- Eligible salary ₹10,00,000 and contribution ₹1,00,000 gives a ₹1,00,000 deduction; contribution ₹1,40,000 reaches the cap. A higher contribution must not receive a deduction above the cap. Keep all three contribution amounts included in gross salary.
- Two employers, eligible salary ₹5,00,000 each and contribution ₹70,000 each, gives a combined ₹1,40,000 deduction and only one standard deduction. Repeated earlier-employer amounts must not be accepted as extra income or contributions.
- An over-cap first employer and an under-cap second employer distinguishes per-employer clipping from pooled clipping. Choose and document the support boundary before implementing this test.
- Low aggregate income must floor taxable income at zero by limiting the NPS deduction to gross total income, while allowing available freelance income to absorb a valid deduction beyond taxable salary.
- Pre-deduction income ₹4,20,000 and valid deduction ₹30,000 still requires a return, even though taxable income is ₹3,90,000 and tax is nil.
- Test rebate and marginal-relief boundaries and a pre-deduction amount above ₹50 lakh reduced to the supported taxable ceiling.
- Missing or uncertain scope, unsafe sums, negative values, stale or incomplete core Rules and salary/contribution contradictions must not produce an estimate.
- Exactly ₹7,50,000 of aggregate employer fund contributions fits the proposed scope; any excess does not. Prior-year taxable accretion remains outside even where this year's total is below that threshold.
- Clearing salary or employer NPS must remove inactive amounts and confirmations. Migration must not invent a contribution or new consent, and saved results must recalculate under current Rules.

## Rule review and remaining uncertainty

Record verification date 2026-09-07 and applicability 2026-04-01 through 2027-03-31. Reuse the existing core income-tax group and its review expiry, presently 2027-08-31. Expiry is a product review deadline, not a statutory sunset. Missing or expired NPS authority must fail closed with core income tax. Do not borrow the independent GST group's shorter review date.

S1 planning finding: using taxable income after employer NPS for annual-return eligibility would remove a required obligation. Preserve a separate before-deduction amount in the shared evaluator and screening path.

S2 planning finding: a pooled employer salary/contribution total does not resolve cross-employer over-cap treatment. Preserve per-employer evidence and use the stricter support boundary above if that interpretation cannot be confirmed before release.

No other findings against the proposed bounded input contract. The fund-threshold confirmation avoids implementing the unresolved valuation details of current or earlier excess contributions.
