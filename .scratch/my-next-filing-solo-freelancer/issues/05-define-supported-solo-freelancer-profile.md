# Define the supported solo-freelancer Profile

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 02, 04, 16

## Question

Based on the statutory findings and the settled release boundary, what exact declared facts define each supported resident-individual Solo freelancer Profile? Decide which professions, domestic and foreign client arrangements, freelance platforms, GST states, income methods, receipt limits, currency facts, and unsupported conditions produce a Supported result, a partial result, or an Unsupported result. Name the canonical terms and update the domain glossary as they settle.

## Answer

Use the domain terms **Solo freelancer**, **Specified professional path**, **Eligible business path**, **Foreign professional receipts**, and **Incomplete coverage** recorded in `CONTEXT.md`. A job title never selects a statutory path.

### Shared Profile boundary

Every calculable Profile must confirm that the user:

- is an individual aged eighteen or older;
- is resident and ordinarily resident in India for the Tax Year;
- uses the new tax regime;
- operates one self-employed service practice set up and managed in India;
- performs all income-producing work in India;
- has no partner, employee, foreign operation, or subcontractor delivering the client work;
- uses exactly one confirmed presumptive path for the whole practice;
- has no audit requirement, unsupported income, deduction, loss, credit, special-rate item, surcharge case, or taxable total income above ₹50 lakh; and
- can provide complete integer-rupee amounts from their tax records.

An incidental domestic contractor may remain within scope only when the contractor does not deliver the client work and creates no payroll, deductor, agency, second-business, or foreign-operation duty. An uncertain answer stops calculation.

Support domestic-only, foreign-only, and mixed domestic-and-foreign clients. Support direct and platform-mediated work only within the gates below.

### Income-path choice

The user must explicitly confirm the path used in their tax records or chosen with professional advice. The Application explains the paths but does not infer one from an occupation label. “Not sure” stops calculation.

Offer these plain-language activity choices:

- Software development and information technology.
- Technical consultancy.
- Design.
- Writing and content.
- Marketing and advertising.
- Another digital service.

Information technology and technical consultancy may use the Specified professional path after confirmation. The remaining choices require Eligible business path confirmation. “Another digital service” calculates only when the user confirms that the whole practice is one eligible business and is not a specified profession, agency, commission, brokerage, goods, royalty, or licensing activity.

Closely related activities may share one Profile only when the user confirms that every activity belongs to the same practice and path. Mixed specified-profession and eligible-business work stops calculation.

### Specified professional path

This path requires:

- confirmation that the whole practice is a section 62 specified profession;
- gross receipts before expenses, platform fees, Indian withholding, or other deductions;
- cash receipts, with non-account-payee cheques and drafts treated as cash;
- gross receipts no higher than ₹50 lakh when cash receipts exceed 5%, or ₹75 lakh when cash receipts do not exceed 5%; and
- declared profit no lower than 50% of gross receipts.

A lower-profit claim, unknown cash amount, exceeded limit, regular-books case, audit case, or uncertain profession classification stops this path.

### Eligible business path

This path requires confirmation that:

- the whole practice is an eligible business and not a section 62 specified profession;
- it is not goods carriage, agency, commission, or brokerage;
- no Chapter VIII-C deduction is claimed;
- no five-year presumptive-method exclusion applies;
- gross receipts can be separated into qualifying banking or online receipts and all other receipts;
- gross receipts are no higher than ₹2 crore, or ₹3 crore when the statutory low-cash condition applies; and
- declared profit is no lower than the calculated 6%/8% presumptive minimum.

An uncertain classification, payment-mode split, five-year history, deduction, exclusion, lower-profit claim, exceeded limit, books case, or audit case stops this path. The Eligible business path is not a fallback when the Specified professional path fails.

### Receipts and platform work

Use gross consideration for the freelancer's service before platform fees and Indian withholding. Do not reverse-engineer gross receipts from a net payout.

Platform work is supported only when the user confirms that:

- they supply the main service on their own account;
- their records identify the contractual recipient;
- their records state the gross customer charge before platform fees and withholding;
- the payment is not employment income, commission, brokerage, royalty, licensing income, or an agency receipt; and
- a foreign platform fee has known GST treatment and creates no unsupported recipient-side reverse-charge duty.

An unclear gross amount, recipient, legal relationship, or income character stops calculation. A possible foreign-held account or balance follows Incomplete coverage when the tax arithmetic is otherwise established. Provider brands never control classification.

### Foreign professional receipts

When a foreign client or platform exists, require confirmation that:

- the practice and all income-producing work remain in India;
- the overseas contractual recipient is identifiable from the user's records;
- the freelancer supplies the main service on their own account, not as an agent or intermediary;
- the ordinary cross-border place-of-supply rule applies;
- supplier and recipient are not establishments of the same person;
- payment uses convertible foreign exchange or an RBI-permitted rupee route;
- proceeds settle through an authorised route to the user's own Indian bank account for the fully covered path;
- no foreign account, retained platform balance, signing authority, foreign operation, foreign tax, or treaty-relief fact exists for the fully covered path; and
- gross receipts and all currency effects are resolved in the confirmed rupee total.

Accept one annual integer-rupee gross total prepared from the user's tax records under their regular cash or mercantile method. The amount must already handle platform fees, withholding, refunds, chargebacks, receivables, and exchange effects. Do not collect foreign-currency totals or perform conversion.

Foreign clients, foreign currency, or a foreign provider brand alone do not stop calculation. Work outside India, RNOR or uncertain residence, foreign operations, foreign tax or treaty relief, unresolved receipts, receivables, accounts, or exchange effects do stop it.

Do not collect client or platform names, client countries, account numbers, routing details, balances, invoices, documents, or foreign tax identifiers. Ask only the facts needed to select a branch.

### Other income and credits

Continue to support only:

- taxable bank or deposit interest;
- actual Indian TDS for included income;
- actual Indian TCS; and
- advance tax already paid for the Tax Year.

Stop for salary, house property, dividends or gifts, capital gains, crypto, gaming, lottery, agricultural income, unrelated foreign income, foreign tax, disputed credits, deductions, losses, special-rate income, or another business or profession.

### GST branches

- With no GSTIN, complete GST aggregate turnover, and no compulsory-registration fact, return the supported threshold status.
- With one active normal-taxpayer GSTIN, return the income-tax result with Incomplete coverage because the first successor release does not contain the GST-return calendar.
- With another registration state, multiple GSTINs, composition, suspension, cancellation, reverse charge, or uncertain GST facts, keep the income-tax result only when those facts do not alter receipts or the selected income path; make GST coverage unavailable.
- A failed or uncertain export-of-service condition makes GST coverage incomplete when the income-tax facts remain sound. Agency, commission, or unresolved gross receipts stop the calculation because they also break the income path.

The later GST-calendar release may narrow full GST support to one active normal-taxpayer GSTIN in one Indian state.

### Coverage outcomes

**Fully covered:** the selected presumptive path, income-tax arithmetic, supported annual-return boundary, and GST threshold status are all established.

**Incomplete coverage:** income-tax arithmetic is established, but GST, return-form, or foreign-account disclosure guidance is withheld because the unresolved fact does not change that arithmetic. A definite or possible foreign account, wallet, provider-held balance, asset, or signing authority uses this outcome when the tax amounts are otherwise resolved.

**Unsupported calculation:** a fact or uncertainty can change gross receipts, income classification, presumptive eligibility, taxable income, credits, surcharge, audit, foreign tax, or currency effects.

**Stale Rules:** invalid, expired, or incompletely sourced Rules stop every new Evaluation.

Do not select or promise ITR-3 or ITR-4 in the first successor release. Show the supported annual-return Obligation and official starting guidance. The Obligation decision owns any extra age or prescribed-trigger facts, and the Evaluation-model decision owns whether Incomplete coverage becomes a result kind or nested supported sub-results.

### Compliance boundary

The current release remains correct because it rejects the newly supported facts. The successor must implement every stop and Incomplete-coverage boundary above before it may broaden its audience claim.
