Type: task
Status: resolved

## Question

Obtain a qualified Indian privacy review of the planned browser-only saved workspace before the successor specification is approved. The review must settle whether stored income and tax values are financial information under the current SPDI Rules, when browser-only processing is in the operator's possession or control, whether the operator is a Data Fiduciary when the substantive DPDP provisions start, what notice and proof of consent are required, and which safeguard, breach, rights, grievance, retention, deletion, child-user, and Analytics duties apply to a product with no account or user contact identifier. Record approval, required changes, reviewer qualification, review date, and the exact release boundary the opinion covers.
