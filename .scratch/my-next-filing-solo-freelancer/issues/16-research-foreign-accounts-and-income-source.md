# Research foreign payment accounts and income-source classification

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none
Research branch: `research/foreign-accounts-income-source`

## Question

Using current official Indian income-tax, RBI, and other controlling primary sources, when do common freelance-platform balances, payment wallets, virtual receiving accounts, and foreign bank arrangements create a foreign asset, financial interest, account, or signing-authority fact for a resident individual? Separately, when is remote professional work performed in India for a foreign client income from a source outside India for Tax Year 2026-27 return-form and disclosure rules? Identify facts the Application can ask without collecting account identifiers, state what cannot be determined from a provider brand, and define the safe stop or guidance boundary. Write the findings to `../research/foreign-accounts-and-income-source.md` with primary-source citations.

## Answer

[Foreign payment accounts and income-source classification](../research/foreign-accounts-and-income-source.md) finds that a foreign client, currency, or provider brand does not itself establish foreign-source income or a foreign account. The fully supported path requires a resident and ordinarily resident individual whose practice and work are entirely in India, whose proceeds settle through an authorised route into their own Indian bank account, and who has no foreign account, provider-held balance, signing authority, foreign operation, or foreign tax.

A definite or possible foreign account, virtual receiving account, wallet, platform balance, contractual claim against a foreign provider, or signing authority can leave otherwise valid tax arithmetic intact but must suppress ITR-4 and no-foreign-disclosure guidance. Work outside India, foreign operations, RNOR or unknown residence subtype, foreign tax, treaty relief, or unresolved currency and account effects must stop the calculation. Ask legal arrangement and place-of-work questions with a “Not sure” stop path; never classify a provider brand.

Rule 164 already controls ITR-4 eligibility, but the Tax Year 2026-27 ITR-3 form, Schedule FA layout, period, schema, and validation rules need a release-time source check. RBI payment-route permission, FEMA residence, income source, and income-tax foreign-account classification remain separate facts.
