# Home-to-plan copy review

Reviewed 10 September 2026 for a freelancer with very little tax knowledge. Changes are implemented in the existing interface. The review covers Home, Fit, Income and profit, Clients and payments, Taxes and GST, Review, Your plan, their help dialogs, and shared validation, saving and deletion messages.

The interaction contract is: a freelancer can enter confirmed facts and full-year amounts to see an estimate and supported actions; answers remain in the browser, can be edited, and do not submit anything to the government.

The main problems were undefined tax language, questions that referred to later “supported cards”, and instructions that could encourage a person to select a favourable answer just to continue. The revisions teach terms at the decision, distinguish uncertainty from an input error, and explain what each action actually does. Technical names remain where people need to match their records.

This is a copy and presentation change. The evaluator's conditions, tax arithmetic, source identities and rule dataset are unchanged. Existing tests were updated only where their assertions or accessible locators referenced replaced copy. A focused browser case checks the new filing-conditions dialog and focus return without changing answers.

There is still a product constraint words cannot remove: the form requires confirmed tax residence, a tax method and some legal classifications. It cannot determine these from everyday facts. The copy now teaches the terms and makes uncertainty safe; it does not imply a first-time filer can establish every classification without records or professional help. In particular, the tax-method choice still has no Not sure radio, so its help explicitly says to leave it unselected if unknown. Adding a residence or classification interview would be a separate behaviour change.
