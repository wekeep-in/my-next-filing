## Input contract

Require an explicit none, uncertain or domestic branch. Domestic contains a confirmation and two non-negative safe-integer annual gain amounts. Require all transactions and all brokers/funds to be included once, with ownership, holding periods, costs, eligible expenses and any pre-February-2018 basis already resolved. Amounts are gains, not sale proceeds, bank credits or portfolio appreciation. Enter long-term gains before the annual ₹1,25,000 threshold or basic-exemption adjustment. STT is not an extra deduction. Confirm sale STT for short-term gains; acquisition and sale STT for long-term shares; transfer STT for long-term fund units. Notification-based acquisition exceptions and IFSC exceptions are outside this initial branch.

No current-year or brought-forward losses, loss set-off, capital-gain exemption/reinvestment claims, foreign or unlisted shares, derivatives, intraday/business trading, debt or nonqualifying funds, REIT/InvIT/AIF/ULIP income, property, employee shares, buybacks, clubbing or unresolved corporate actions. None of these exclusions asserts that the transaction is unlawful. Existing generic capital-gain and special-rate stop facts are retained until explicitly reviewed.

## Calculation

Compute supported ordinary income as before. Cap employer NPS at that ordinary income, excluding both gain categories. Add both full gains to income before NPS and to post-deduction total income, including the portion of long-term gains within ₹1,25,000. That threshold is a tax computation threshold, not an exclusion from total income or return triggers.

Round combined total income once to ₹10. For the tax split, subtract the entered gains to obtain the non-negative ordinary balance; do not independently round every component. With a single positive gain category and ordinary income below ₹4 lakh, its special-rate base is the non-negative rounded combined income above ₹4 lakh; long-term gains additionally use the ₹1,25,000 threshold. Both positive categories with ordinary income below ₹4 lakh need separate review: the reviewed sections do not explicitly settle allocation between them. No invented ordering or double exemption is allowed.

Calculate slab tax on ordinary income; calculate short-term tax at 20% and long-term tax at 12.5% above its threshold. For total income no higher than ₹12 lakh, rebate is the lower of ordinary slab tax and ₹60,000. Above ₹12 lakh, marginal relief is the non-negative excess of total tax before relief over income exceeding ₹12 lakh, capped at ordinary slab tax. This implements section 156(2)(b) with its subsection (3) cap and preserves all special-rate tax. Apply existing cess, credits and final ₹10 rounding. Keep the ₹50 lakh total-income ceiling and all existing practice conditions.

The new Rules and provenance belong to the core common-income-tax group, expire on its existing 31 August 2027 review deadline, and fail closed if missing, invalid or stale. Never copy equity amounts into GST aggregate turnover. Return-form selection stays unavailable; prior assessment-year form guidance does not establish Tax Year 2026-27 eligibility.

## Independent examples

- Ordinary income ₹10 lakh, short-term gains ₹1 lakh, long-term gains ₹2 lakh: total ₹13 lakh; slab tax ₹40,000, short-term tax ₹20,000, long-term tax ₹9,375, no relief, cess ₹2,775, gross tax ₹72,150.
- Ordinary income ₹9 lakh and short-term gains ₹1 lakh: slab tax ₹30,000 is rebated, special-rate tax ₹20,000 remains; including cess ₹20,800.
- Ordinary income ₹3 lakh and short-term gains ₹2 lakh: unused basic exemption ₹1 lakh; tax including cess ₹20,800.
- Ordinary income ₹3 lakh and long-term gains ₹3 lakh: after ₹1 lakh basic-exemption adjustment and ₹1.25 lakh threshold, ₹75,000 attracts 12.5%; including cess ₹9,750.
- Ordinary income ₹11 lakh and short-term gains ₹1.1 lakh: combined tax ₹72,000, excess income ₹10,000, marginal relief capped at ₹50,000 slab tax; special-rate tax remains ₹22,000, including cess ₹22,880.
