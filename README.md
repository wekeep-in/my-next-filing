# My Next Filing

I built this because compliance reminders gave me plenty of deadlines and little help deciding what to do next.

My Next Filing is a static website for supported Solo freelancers in India. Enter your facts to get an income-tax estimate and a chronological plan of supported actions. One main card shows what to handle next. You file and pay outside the application. It does not send reminders or connect to government systems.

## What it covers

The current application covers Tax Year 2026-27 for resident individuals using one confirmed presumptive income path. Supported cases can include domestic salary alongside freelancing, bank interest, and Indian tax credits.

The plan includes applicable advance-tax and annual-return actions, a GST registration check, and a GST return calendar for supported registered freelancers. LUT guidance for eligible service exports has separate coverage. The questionnaire explains when your facts fall outside the supported scope.

A fictional example lets you try the journey before entering your own answers. Resources lets you search official references without completing a Profile.

## Browser data

The application does not upload your answers or amounts, and it has no Analytics or session replay. A Recovery draft can restore personal answers after a refresh in the current tab. Closing the tab may remove it.

You can explicitly save a supported Profile and the Completion dates you add in the current browser. Anyone sharing that browser profile may see the data. Clearing site data or using private browsing may remove it. There is no account, sync, or backup.

Saved answers receive a fresh Evaluation when reopened. Calculated results are never stored. Completion dates record what you say you did. The application cannot verify government acceptance.

## Sources and limits

Rules are local, versioned, sourced, and subject to review deadlines. Invalid or stale core Rules stop calculation. An unavailable independent area withholds only its affected conclusions and actions.
